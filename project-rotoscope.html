Production Workflow

1

Source Selection

Choose high-quality video with clear subject-background contrast and interesting movement patterns for optimal rotoscoping.

2

Pre-Production

Cut the source video into 5-second clips using CapCut for more manageable editing and faster workflow iteration.

3

Composition Setup

Create separate 5-second compositions in After Effects for each segment with proper settings and configurations.

4

Frame-by-Frame Tracing

Hand-draw rotoscope paths with precision masking tools, ensuring accurate subject isolation frame by frame.

5

Color & Fill

Apply solid color fills to each isolated layer and component for visual clarity and professional appearance.

6

Rendering & Export

Export each composition through the Render Queue with consistent settings for seamless final compilation.
